STYLE RULES: SCIENCE SHORTS

FORMAT
- Vertical 9:16 short, about 75 seconds.
- Narration: 165 to 185 words. 16 to 20 scenes, 3 to 5 seconds each.
- Hard cuts only. No fades. No scene longer than 6 seconds.

STORY STRUCTURE (share of total time)
1. Hook, 0 to 7%: an alarming "What if" premise in the first 2 sentences,
   with a contrast. Example shape: "For one second, X happens. And you don't."
2. Premise, 7 to 17%: one fact that sets up the stakes, with a number.
3. Main event, 17 to 40%: what happens step by step, one physical law per
   step, shown at the moment its effect appears.
4. Second event, 40 to 55%: something that seems to make it worse.
5. Personal stake, 55 to 80%: follow "you" in one specific place (a seat,
   a room, a bunker). Contrast: the safest place is not where you think.
6. Twist, 80 to 94%: a delayed or secondary consequence that arrives after
   you believe you are safe. Ironic, quiet tone.
7. Closing, 94 to 100%: one direct question to the viewer. No "subscribe".

WRITING
- Second person ("you"), present tense.
- 8 to 14 words per sentence. Short linking words: "Because", "So when",
  "Meanwhile", "Except", "Then".
- Real science only. Every number must be real. If unsure, leave it out.
- Put a 0.5 to 1 second pause before each big reveal by ending the sentence
  before it with a full stop.
- The ending line is understated and darkly ironic, then the question.

NARRATOR
- Male, early 30s, neutral accent, dynamic documentary delivery.
- About 145 words per minute, never above 160.
- Tone curve: intriguing, then urgent, then deadpan irony.

VISUALS
- Flat minimalist 2D vector. No outlines, no drop shadows, no 3D.
- Characters: pill-shaped body, oversized round head, large glowing
  cyan/white oval eyes, no mouth. 20 to 35 percent of screen height.
- Backgrounds: deep navy space with starfield, or a two-colour sky
  gradient. No complex gradients.
- Palette: navy #0B1021, deep navy #080B14, electric cyan #22D3EE,
  sky blue #60A5FA, gold #FBBF24, coral #F87171, white #FFFFFF.
- Recurring motifs: a digital timer fixed in the top-right corner, pill
  tags for places, gauges for numbers, small inset map for locations.
- Motion: smooth ease-in/ease-out, snappy pop-in for labels, slight bob on
  idle objects, slow push-in or pull-back camera.

TEXT ON SCREEN
- ALL CAPS, bold geometric sans-serif, white, key word in gold.
- 1 to 4 words per label, one label at a time.
- Number badges such as "35,000 FT", "800 MPH".

SOUND
- Low ambient synth drone under the voice, ducked about 12 dB.
- Effects: whoosh, sub-bass drop, boom, tick, chime, rumble. Keep them
  crisp and quiet under the voice. A sudden silence after a big drop.
- Never cartoon sounds.

NEVER
- Photorealistic or 3D scenes, gore, real people or brands.
- Static shots over 8 seconds, paragraphs of text, serif or script fonts.
- A tidy happy ending: always end with a twist or a question.
